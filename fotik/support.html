Fotik

“I deleted photos but my storage did not change”

This is how iOS works, not a bug in Fotik. Deleted photos go to the system album Recently Deleted and stay there for 30 days, still taking up space. No app can empty that album for you. Open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted and empty it — the space is freed immediately.

“Fotik found fewer photos than I have”

If you granted limited photo access, Fotik only sees the photos you selected. Grant full access in Settings → Fotik → Photos, or tap the banner in the app to select more.

“The size shown is smaller than expected”

Photos whose originals live in iCloud take up much less space on the device itself. Fotik does not count them towards the promise, so the number you see is the space you will actually get back.

Restore a purchase

Open the Pro screen and tap “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
