What the app does

Fotik finds duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos, large videos and other clutter in your photo library and helps you delete what you choose. Nothing is deleted without your confirmation.

How deleting works on iOS. When you delete photos, iOS moves them to the system album “Recently Deleted”, where they remain for 30 days and continue to take up space. No third-party app is allowed to empty that album for you. Fotik tells you this and guides you to the second step; space is freed once you empty “Recently Deleted” yourself, or after 30 days.

Free use and Fotik Pro

Scanning and deleting are free and unlimited. Fotik Pro is optional and speeds up the work: selecting everything at once, smart cleanup across all categories, and bulk “keep the best” in duplicate groups.
