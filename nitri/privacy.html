Privacy Policy

Nitri · Last updated September 8, 2026

Nitri collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no server of ours, and no analytics. Everything you type stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your tanks: names, dimensions, volume, set-up date, tap-water nitrate.
  • Your readings: ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, hardness, temperature, dates and notes.
  • Your inhabitants and your care log — water changes, doses, cleanings.
  • Photos you attach to a tank, kept inside the app's own storage.
  • Your answers to the setup questions and your unit preferences.

None of it leaves the phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no copy anywhere else, which also means there is no way for us to restore it.

What the app does not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no ATT prompt, because there is nothing to track.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No contacts, no location, no health data.
  • The species book is bundled with the app and is read offline.

Camera and photo library

Permission is requested only when you attach a picture to a tank, and the picture is copied into the app's own storage. Nothing is uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether an entitlement is active — nothing more. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

Nitri is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it.
