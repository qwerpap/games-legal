Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com. Replies usually go out within a couple of days. The answers below cover most of what people ask.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On your device: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Nitri → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends, or it renews. Deleting the app does not cancel anything — the subscription lives in your Apple Account, not in the app.

I paid on another device and Pro is not on here

Open Nitri → Me → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Why is the water volume smaller than my tank's rating?

Because gravel, rocks, wood and the gap under the rim are not water. Nitri takes the volume of the glass and multiplies it by how much of that is actually water — 0.85 by default, and you can change it on the tank's screen. Every dose and every water change is counted from the water, not from the box, which is why they come out different from what the bottle's "per tank size" table suggests.

Why does the app want two clean tests before it says the tank is cycled?

Because one clean test happens for reasons other than a mature filter — a large water change the day before, for instance. Two readings a day apart with no ammonia, no nitrite and nitrate on the board is the smallest evidence that does not lie.

Where do the "days left" come from?

From two independent places, shown side by side and never averaged. One is how long that stage usually takes. The other extends your own nitrite readings with a straight line and says when they reach the bottom step of a drop test, plus two days for the confirming test. When the two disagree, that itself is worth knowing: your tank is running faster or slower than most.

Can Nitri tell me what is wrong with my fish?

No, and it will not pretend to. It does arithmetic on the numbers you type. It cannot see the animal, cannot measure your water and does not prescribe treatments. For a sick animal, ask a vet or an experienced keeper.

Does my data go anywhere?

No. There is no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy.

I found a mistake in the species book

Please write, and say which species and which number. The ranges are deliberately cautious, but they are compiled from published hobby sources and those disagree with each other more often than people expect.
