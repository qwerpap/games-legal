What Nitri is

Nitri is a logbook for aquariums. You enter the readings from your own test kit — ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, pH, hardness, temperature — and Nitri does arithmetic on them: it works out which stage of the nitrogen cycle your readings describe, how many millilitres of a product your tank's water volume needs, how many litres to change to reach a nitrate target, and whether a species you are considering fits the water you measured.

It is arithmetic on numbers you typed, not advice and not a diagnosis. Nitri cannot measure water. It has no way of knowing what is really in your tank beyond what you enter, it does not know the product in your hand, and it never decides whether to treat an animal or how. Every calculation is shown with the working underneath it precisely so you can check it before you act on it. If an animal is sick, ask a vet or an experienced keeper.

Dosing

The dose calculator rescales the instruction printed on your bottle to the volume of water your tank actually holds. It contains no database of products and no dosages of its own, and it cannot tell whether the figure you typed is the right one. Read the label. Overdosing kills fish faster than most of the problems people dose for.

The species book

Ranges for temperature, pH, hardness, adult size and minimum tank volume are compiled from widely published hobby sources and deliberately err on the cautious side. Sources disagree; animals vary; a range is a guide, not a guarantee. Entries where the sources disagree most are marked as such in the app.
