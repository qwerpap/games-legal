Privacy Policy

Cairn · Last updated September 6, 2026

Cairn identifies rocks and minerals from a photo. This page says plainly what happens to that photo and to everything else the app holds.

The photo you take

To name a stone, Cairn sends the picture to its identification service. This is the one thing that leaves your phone, and it only happens when you ask for an identification.

  • The image is resized before it is sent and is used only to produce the answer.
  • It is not kept after the answer is returned, and it is not used to train anything.
  • No name, email, account or device identifier is attached to it. We do not have accounts, and Cairn never asks you to create one.
  • The field guide, the field tests and everything already in your collection work with no network at all.

Your collection

Specimens you keep — the photo, the name, the date, your notes and the place you typed — are stored on your device only. They are not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not visible to anyone but you. Deleting a specimen deletes its photo from the device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What Cairn does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. Cairn does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to track you with.
  • No location access. If you want to record where a stone came from, you type it in; the app never reads your position.
  • No access to your contacts, calendar, microphone or health data.

The photo library

If you choose a picture instead of taking one, iOS hands Cairn only the picture you picked. The app cannot browse your library.

Subscriptions

Cairn Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether the subscription is active. We never see your card, your Apple Account, or your billing address.

Children

Cairn is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that would identify anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
