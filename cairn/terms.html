What Cairn is

Cairn suggests what a rock or mineral might be from a photograph, and gives you the physical tests that narrow the list down. It is a field aid for collectors and the curious.

It is not an authority. A photograph cannot distinguish some minerals from one another, and neither can this app. Cairn answers with a shortlist and confidence rather than a verdict for exactly that reason. Do not rely on it for a purchase, a sale, an insurance valuation, a safety decision, or anything else that matters. For that, ask a geologist or a lab.

Worth estimates

Price bands shown in the app are rough guides for typical hand specimens. Condition, size, locality and the market move a real price far more than the name does. Nothing in Cairn is an appraisal or an offer.

Safety

Some minerals are hazardous. Where Cairn knows of a hazard it says so, but its notes are general and its identification can be wrong. Handle unknown material accordingly: do not grind, heat, taste or inhale dust from it, and use household vinegar rather than anything stronger for the acid test.
