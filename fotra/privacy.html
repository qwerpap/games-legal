Fotra

Privacy Policy · Last updated August 27, 2026
Short version: Fotra has no accounts and no tracking. Your photos and edits stay on your device. The only exception is AI effects: if you use one, that single photo is sent to a processing service, used to produce the result, and not stored afterwards.

Information we collect

None directly. Fotra does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. There is no sign-in, no profile and no analytics SDK in the app.

Your photos

Photos you open are copied into the app's own storage so your work survives changes in your photo library. Everything you do — crops, colour, retouch, brush strokes, text — is stored on your device only, as a description of the edits rather than as a flattened image. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Fotra asks for photo library access only when you open a photo, and for add-only access when you save a result. It never scans your library in the background.

AI effects

Effects marked as AI are not computed on your phone. When you start one, the selected photo is uploaded over an encrypted connection to a processing service, transformed, and returned to your device. The photo is used for that request only and is not kept, sold or used to train anything. If you never open an AI effect, no photo ever leaves your device.

Camera

The in-app camera is used only when you open it, to take a photo you then edit. Nothing is recorded or transmitted while the camera preview is on screen.

Subscriptions

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details. Apple tells the app only whether an active subscription exists.

Children

Fotra is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
