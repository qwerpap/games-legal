Privacy Policy

Glyphra · Last updated October 4, 2026

Glyphra reads text from photographs of pages. All of that work happens on your device. There are no accounts, and your images and the text taken from them are never uploaded — not to us and not to anyone else. The app goes online only to handle purchases and your subscription.

The short version. Your pages and their text never leave your iPhone. To sell and restore subscriptions, Glyphra uses Adapty, which receives a random device identifier, your purchase history and basic in-app events (such as when the subscription screen is shown). This data is not linked to your identity and is never used for tracking or advertising. There is no advertising SDK and no crash reporter.

Camera and photos

Glyphra asks for the camera so you can photograph a page, and for your photo library so you can pick a picture you already took.

  • Images are handled entirely on your iPhone.
  • Recognition uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs locally.
  • Glyphra never uploads a photo, a page, or recognized text anywhere.
  • Photos you pick are copied into the app only when you save a scan.

What stays on your device

The scans you keep — page images, the recognized text, titles, folders and dates — are stored inside the app on your iPhone. They are included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone, and they are deleted when you delete a scan or delete the app.

Translation

The translation feature uses Apple's system translation. Language packs are downloaded once by iOS itself, and the translation runs on your device. Glyphra does not send the text to a translation service.

Purchases and subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details.

To show current prices and offers and to check whether your subscription is active, Glyphra uses Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc.). Adapty receives:

  • a random identifier generated for this installation (not your Apple ID and not the advertising identifier);
  • your App Store purchase and subscription history for Glyphra;
  • basic app events, such as when the subscription screen is shown and which onboarding step you reached.

We use this only to run the subscription and to understand which screens work. It is not linked to your name or email, not sold, and not used to track you across other apps or websites. Page images, recognized text, titles and folders are never sent to Adapty.

Children

Glyphra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
