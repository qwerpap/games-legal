Privacy Policy

Glyphra · Last updated September 6, 2026

Glyphra reads text from photographs of pages. All of that work happens on your device. The app has no servers, no accounts, and makes no network requests of its own — your images and the text taken from them are never uploaded, including to us.

The short version. We collect nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no tracking of any kind in Glyphra. Nothing about you or your documents reaches us.

Camera and photos

Glyphra asks for the camera so you can photograph a page, and for your photo library so you can pick a picture you already took.

  • Images are handled entirely on your iPhone.
  • Recognition uses Apple's Vision framework, which runs locally.
  • Glyphra never uploads a photo, a page, or recognized text anywhere.
  • Photos you pick are copied into the app only when you save a scan.

What stays on your device

The scans you keep — page images, the recognized text, titles, folders and dates — are stored inside the app on your iPhone. They are included in your device backup if you back up your iPhone, and they are deleted when you delete a scan or delete the app.

Translation

The translation feature uses Apple's system translation. Language packs are downloaded once by iOS itself, and the translation runs on your device. Glyphra does not send the text to a translation service.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether a subscription is active. We never see your payment details, and we do not receive an identifier that would let us recognise you.

Children

Glyphra is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
