Support

Lume · Last updated September 7, 2026

Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you are on — it usually saves a round trip.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lume → Cancel. Cancelling stops the next charge; the current period runs to its end. Cancelling during the free trial means nothing is charged at all. This is handled by Apple, so it cannot be done from inside the app.

Restore a purchase

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Open Me in Lume and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account that bought the subscription.

It named my watch wrong

Expected, and it is why the app answers with three. Two things help more than a second photo: shoot the dial straight on and fill the frame, and then type the numbers engraved on the case back — that search runs across the whole book and finds models the photo missed. After that, run the marking checks the result screen offers — the calibre, the case width and the bezel rule out candidates that a photograph never could. If it is still wrong, send us the photo and what you think it is; that is how the book gets better.

The identification won't run

It needs a connection. The reference book, your collection, the engraving search and every marking check work offline, so you can run the tests now and identify later.

A photo disappeared

Photos live in the app's own storage. Deleting a watch deletes its photo, and deleting the app deletes all of them — neither can be undone from our side.

Delete your data

Everything Lume holds is on your device. Delete the app and it is gone. We hold no account and no copy.
