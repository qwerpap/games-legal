Support

Recoup · Last updated September 8, 2026

Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com. Please say which iPhone and which iOS version you are on — it usually saves a round trip.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Recoup → Cancel. Cancelling stops the next charge; the current period runs to its end. Cancelling during a free trial means nothing is charged at all. This is handled by Apple, so it cannot be done from inside the app.

Restore a purchase

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Open Recoup's settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account that bought Recoup Pro or the lifetime unlock.

The value or sub-limit numbers look wrong

Recoup only does arithmetic on what you typed in — the price, the purchase date, the quantity. Check those first; a wrong purchase date is the most common cause of a value looking off. The sub-limit figures come from a standard policy form, not from your policy, so if your insurer's limits differ, the app has no way to know that — it can only tell you what a typical policy would cap.

A photo or an item disappeared

Everything lives in the app's own storage. Deleting an item deletes its photo, and deleting the app deletes all of it — neither can be undone from our side, because we never hold a copy.

Delete your data

Everything Recoup holds is on your device. Delete the app and it is gone. We hold no account and no copy.
