Privacy Policy

Recoup · Last updated September 8, 2026

Recoup is a home inventory for insurance purposes: you list your rooms and what's in them, and the app works out what that adds up to. This page says plainly what happens to that list.

Nothing you enter ever leaves your phone. Recoup makes no network requests at all. There is no account, no login, and no server on the other end to send anything to.

What Recoup stores

Every room and every item — name, category, price, purchase date, quantity, serial number — is written to a database that lives in the app's own storage on your device. Photos of an item or its receipt are saved the same way, in the app's own folder. None of it is uploaded, synced to us, or visible to anyone but you.

Photos

Recoup only sees a photo when you hand it one: by taking a picture, or by picking one from your library. Choosing from the library uses Apple's picker, which gives the app just the picture you selected — Recoup cannot browse the rest of your library. A photo you attach is stored on the device like everything else; deleting the item deletes its photo.

What Recoup does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. Recoup does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it has nothing to track you with.
  • No accounts, so no name, email or password is ever collected.
  • No location access, no contacts, no calendar, no microphone, no health data.
  • No servers of ours are involved anywhere in the app — the numbers you see are computed on the device, from what you typed in.

Purchases

Recoup Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether the subscription or lifetime unlock is active. We never see your card, your Apple Account, or your billing address.

Deleting your data

Delete a room or an item and its record and photo are gone immediately. Delete the app and the entire database and every photo go with it — we hold no copy, because we were never sent one.

Children

Recoup is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing that would identify anyone of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
