What Recoup is

Recoup lets you record what you own — room by room, item by item — and adds it up. It applies a standard depreciation curve to what you paid so you can see a rough current value, and it flags where the standard sub-limits printed in a typical homeowners or renters policy (jewelry, firearms, silverware, bicycles, and the like) would cap what a claim actually pays, compared with the coverage limit you told it you carry. It is a record-keeping and arithmetic tool for your own use, built for one thing: making a claim easier if you ever need to file one.

Recoup is not insurance, and it is not insurance advice. It is not a broker, an adjuster, an appraiser, or a substitute for one. It does arithmetic on the numbers you type in — the sub-limits it shows are figures taken from a standard policy form, not from your actual policy, which may set different amounts or none at all. Whether your coverage is enough, and what your policy actually pays out, are decided by the policy you hold and the insurer who wrote it — not by this app. Talk to your insurer or an agent before making a coverage decision, and treat anything Recoup shows as a starting point for that conversation, never as the answer.
