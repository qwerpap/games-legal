Common questions

Where are my edits? Open the "Works" tab. Every photo you edited is there, and every edit is still adjustable — nothing is baked in.

An AI effect failed. Some photos are rejected by the processing model's content rules. Try a different effect or a different photo; you are not charged for a failed effect.

Face tools do nothing. They need a visible face in the frame. On photos without one, the panel says so directly.

How do I cancel the subscription? Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Flare → Cancel. Cancelling keeps access until the end of the paid period.

I restored my purchase and nothing happened. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the purchase, then tap Restore Purchases again in the Profile tab.
