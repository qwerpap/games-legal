Common questions

A barcode is not recognized

Shelvy looks products up in Open Food Facts, a volunteer database. It covers Europe better than the United States, and new products appear in it late. Add the item by hand — it takes two taps — and it will be there next time.

The receipt was read wrong

Receipts differ from shop to shop, and thermal print fades. Shelvy always shows what it read as a list you confirm before anything is added, so you can fix a line or drop it. Good light and a flat receipt help.

Where do the expiry dates come from

From the date you enter, or from a typical storage time for that category when you do not enter one. They are reminders, not a safety verdict — judge food by looking and smelling, and follow the date printed on the package.

I paid and Pro is not on

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to the Apple Account that bought them, so make sure you are signed in with that one.

How do I cancel

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment and never renews.
