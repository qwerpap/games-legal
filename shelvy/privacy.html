Privacy Policy

Shelvy · Last updated September 7, 2026

Your food list never leaves your phone. Items, prices, photos and receipts are stored on the device and are not uploaded to us. There is no account and no login.

What stays on your device

  • Everything you add: items, quantities, prices, dates, locations, notes.
  • Photos you take and receipts you scan. A receipt is read by Apple's on-device text recognition; the image is not sent anywhere.
  • Your settings and reminders.

If you turn on iCloud in iOS, this data is synced through your own iCloud account under Apple's terms. We have no access to it.

What leaves the device

  • Barcode lookups. When you scan a barcode, the number is sent to Open Food Facts to fetch the product name, brand and image. Only the barcode is sent. Nothing about you or the rest of your list is included.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Anonymous diagnostics. Crash reports and basic usage counts, through Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics, so we can find bugs. These contain no item names, no prices, no photos and no receipts.

What we do not do

We do not track you across apps or websites, do not build an advertising profile, do not sell data, and do not show ads. Shelvy asks for no contacts, no location and no health data.

Permissions

  • Camera — to read a barcode or a receipt. Frames are processed on the device and are not stored unless you save the photo.
  • Notifications — to remind you before food expires. Optional; the app works without them.

Children

Shelvy is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you control it directly: delete an item, or delete the app, and it is gone. For questions about diagnostics, write to us through Support.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
